## Operator Precedence

Here are some common operators in C, from highest to lowest priority:

1. () 
Parentheses

1. *, /, % 
Multiplication, Division, Modulus

1. +, - 
Addition, Subtraction

1. >, <, >=, <= 
Comparison

1. ==, != 
Equality

1. && 
Logical AND

1. || 
Logical OR

1. = 
Assignment


```c
int result1 = 2 + 3 * 4;     // 2 + 12 = 14
int result2 = (2 + 3) * 4;   // 5 * 4 = 20

printf("%d\n", result1);
printf("%d\n", result2);
```
